No contracts, fine-print surprises, or hidden fees.
No contracts.
We want you to use our services because you love them, not because you’re locked in. We don’t use contracts, so you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime. And it’s easy to cancel — you can call us or you can do it online.
No AI answering.
PATLive does not use AI to answer calls, so all your callers always receive friendly, personal service from a live human being — not a frustrating and impersonal experience with an AI bot.
No offshore or outsourcing.
We answer 100% of calls right here in the US with our own award-winning staff. No matter how busy things get, you never have to worry that any of your calls could be sent to outside “partners,” overseas or here at home.
No off-phone billing.
We believe you should only pay for the time receptionists are live on your calls, and that’s exactly how we bill. We don’t charge for after-call notes, message entry, or wrap-up, which can inflate billable time by minutes per call.
No per feature fees.
Per-feature fees may seem like small amounts at first glance, but they can really add up fast. Our all-inclusive pricing covers all our live capabilities, so no matter what our team does on your calls, your price stays the same.
No night or weekend fees.
24/7 service isn’t just something we offer, it’s included in every plan. No “core weekday hours” with extra charges if you want coverage on nights and weekends — just true 24/7/365 service, even on holidays.
No bilingual fees.
You can take advantage of our bilingual service for your Spanish-speaking callers at no extra cost. We even include a free auto attendant so you can have callers press 1 for English or 2 for Spanish.
No change fees.
Your business changes, and your call handling should keep up. Update your greetings, call flows, and call handling instructions as often as you like, and upgrade, downgrade or cancel your plan at any time with no fees or penalties.
Our fair and transparent pricing is just one of many reasons why our customers love us!

How does the free trial work?
Your free trial is a full-feature test drive of PATLive’s live call answering service. There are no limitations, so you can experience everything PATLive has to offer from day one.
What happens after my free trial ends?
Your service continues without interruption. Your calls will be answered exactly as they were during your trial, and your selected plan will begin. There are no surprises or changes to the way your calls are handled (unless you’d like to make changes, of course – those are always welcome, just let us know).
How do I get my calls to PATLive?
When you signup, we’ll assign your choice of a local or toll-free number. You can advertise this number directly (you own it and can port it out at any time), or you can simply forward your calls to this number.
You can also port your number into our platform. Some customers choose to do this so that they can use our cloud phone platform to place calls and send texts from the same number that customers are calling in on, and/or to take advantage of some of the more advanced call routing features it offers.
How will I receive the information you collect from callers?
You’ll be updated after every call by email, text message, our apps, or through an integration with software you already use. For more details, see How It Works
.
Can calls ring to me first and then roll over to PATLive if I don’t answer?
Yes, we can help you easily configure ring-no-answer forwarding from your current number.
Or if you’re using a number that’s hosted on our platform, we can help you set up even more advanced options – our call management platform is essentially a fully featured cloud phone system that you get for free with all our plans.
Can you handle calls differently for each of my locations or teams?
Yes. We’re happy to customize call handling for different people, teams, locations, or businesses within a single account. Whether it’s a different greeting for each location, tailored scripts for sales vs. customer support, or unique messaging for individual team members, every caller gets the right experience — without you having to manage multiple accounts.
My calls will never be sent to voicemail unless I ask you to, right?
Correct. 100% of your calls will be answered by one of our live receptionists, and we will never send your callers to voicemail unless you direct us to.
However, sometimes customers want their callers to be able to leave a voicemail instead of relaying a message, so we offer both options.
How does the free trial work?
Your free trial is a full-feature test drive of PATLive’s live outbound calling service. There are no limitations, so you can experience everything PATLive has to offer from day one.
What happens after my free trial ends?
Your service continues without interruption. Your calls will be placed exactly as they were during your trial, and your selected plan will begin. There are no surprises or changes to the way your calls are handled (unless you’d like to make changes, of course – those are always welcome, just let us know).
How do I queue new outreaches?
Simply email the request, or spreadsheet of requests, to [email protected]
, and we’ll process them for you.
After I've queued an outreach, how long until the call is placed?
It varies based on current volume, but it’s usually in the range of a few minutes to a few hours at most. We have some things in the works, though, that will be getting this time range down to a consistent 2 to 10 minute range.
How many call attempts will you make per outreach?
For each outreach that you request, we’ll make up to three outbound phone calls to attempt to reach them live. If we haven’t reached them by the third attempt, we’ll leave a voicemail.
We can set the time between attempts based on your preference. By default we make one call per day, but we can also do once an hour or some other interval you specify.
Can I use this for telemarketing?
We can only place calls to people who have opted in to receive marketing calls from you and/or people with whom you have an existing business relationship.
What hours can outbound calls be placed?
You can queue outbound calls any time, but the actual outbound calls will only be placed between the hours of 8am and 6pm ET, Monday through Friday.
Do you have an API that I can use to queue outreaches?
Not yet, but we will soon. In the meantime, if your lead system can email us upon receipt of a new lead, this works well for many of our customers.
How does the free trial work?
Your free trial is a full-feature test drive of PATLive’s live chat service. There are no limitations, so you can experience everything PATLive has to offer from day one.
What happens after my free trial ends?
Your service continues without interruption. Your chats will be answered exactly as they were during your trial, and your selected plan will begin. There are no surprises or changes to the way your chats are handled (unless you’d like to make changes, of course – those are always welcome, just let us know).
How do I add PATLive's chat widget to my website?
Once you’ve completed onboarding, you’ll simply drop our code snippet into the head of your site and you’re ready to go.
Can I customize how the widget looks?
Yep! You can easily modify the widget styling and presentation options to match your website.
This is a 100% human service, right?
Correct. Our web chat widget is supported by real people from the moment a visitor connects. We do not use bots or AI.
And web chats are 24/7, just like call answering, right?
Correct. Just like our 24/7 Live Call Answering service, web chats are 24/7 – even on weekends and holidays.
Can I access the transcripts of my chats?
Absolutely. You’ll receive a notification and summary of each chat, and you can configure this to include the full transcript. Additionally, all of your chats can be viewed in the web app at any time.
How does the free business phone system work?
Our web and mobile apps are designed to support your live services, but they can also serve as a full-featured business phone system. We include the phone system features for free with all our live services, and it’s a completely optional value add —
you do NOT have to use our phone system to use our live services. But if you want to use it, it’s there for you.
Included free:
1 phone number
Unlimited users
Unlimited automated minutes for calls handled by a live receptionist at any point, even after it’s transferred to you
1,000 automated minutes per month for calls that never involve a receptionist
100 text messages per month
If you go beyond that:
$0.02 per additional automated minute or text
$5/mo for each additional phone number
To activate business text messaging, there is a one-time fee of $50 to have your business reviewed and approved by the regulatory body that monitors for potential spam and abuse.
What kind of features does the phone system provide?
The system offers all of the following:
Calling in our mobile app
Texting in our web and mobile apps
Auto attendants
Advanced call routing and forwarding
Users & groups (for teams and departments)
Extensions
Ring groups (to offer calls to multiple people at once)
Voicemail boxes
Contacts
Call recording & reporting
Spam blocking
Local and toll-free phone numbers
Can I use it to replace my existing IP office phones?
Not currently. Our phone system doesn’t connect directly to desk phones, so calls through it are made and answered in our mobile app. But you can layer it on top of your existing phone system to get auto attendants, call routing, call recording & reporting, shared voicemail boxes, etc, then forward calls to your existing office phones and mobile lines.
