Legal intake is how your firm turns callers into clients. A prospective client’s first conversation often decides whether they hire you, which makes intake one of the most important functions in your practice.
Your intake team can’t answer every call, especially when volume spikes or the office is closed. AnswerHero™ provides legal intake services that fill those gaps, so every caller reaches a live professional.

Why Should Law Firms Invest in a Legal Intake Call Center?
24/7 legal intake answering services support busy law offices by handling incoming calls, so your staff can focus on casework. Common reasons firms use a legal intake answering service include:
Busy Attorney Schedules
Between court appearances, client meetings, and case preparation, attorneys have little time to answer the phone. When clients call throughout the day, lawyers are forced to split their attention, reducing overall efficiency.
Consistent Client Interactions
Every client expects prompt attention, regardless of the size of their legal matter. When the office staff is busy and calls go to voicemail, the quality of client experiences might drop. Consistent, professional responses protect your firm’s reputation.
Disaster Recovery Needs
Lawyers handle time-sensitive work related to court cases, claims and settlements. If your office ever becomes unavailable due to severe weather or another emergency, keeping in touch with clients is a necessity. Our agents keep callers informed and take messages until your staff is available.
Lower Staffing Costs
Hiring a new member of your team to handle legal intake is a considerable expense. Between onboarding costs and payroll expenses, this approach might not be achievable for your practice. With AnswerHero’s answering services, you can enjoy call management for a fraction of the cost.
