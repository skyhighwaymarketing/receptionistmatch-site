Common pricing FAQs
How much does an answering service cost with Moneypenny?
Live receptionist plans start at $165 a month for 50 minutes and go up to $985 a month for 500 minutes. AI Receptionist plans start at $99 a month for 50 calls and go up to $775 a month for 500 calls. Every plan includes 24/7 service, with no contract.
Are there extra fees for bilingual answering or call transfers?
No. There are no feature tiers and no upsells. Live receptionist plans include warm call transfers, bilingual answering, 24/7 service, CRM integrations, the mobile app and a free forwarding number from day one. AI Receptionist plans include 24/7 service, a reporting dashboard and human escalation.
What happens if I go over my plan?
Extra minutes on live receptionist plans cost $2.09 to $2.85, depending on your plan. Extra calls on AI Receptionist plans cost $1.69 to $2.39. Larger plans have lower rates, and if you regularly go over, we'll help you move to a plan that fits.
Do I need to sign a long-term contract?
No long-term commitment required. Our plans are flexible, with just 30 days' notice needed to cancel, effective from your next billing date.
Do you charge per minute or per call?
Live receptionist plans include a set number of minutes each month. AI Receptionist plans include a set number of calls. We'll recommend the best-fit plan based on your expected call volume, so you only pay for what you need.
Is an AI receptionist cheaper than a live receptionist?
AI Receptionist plans start at $99 a month, compared with $165 a month for live receptionist plans. Every AI plan includes human escalation, so calls that need a person can be passed to one. You can also combine AI and live receptionists.
