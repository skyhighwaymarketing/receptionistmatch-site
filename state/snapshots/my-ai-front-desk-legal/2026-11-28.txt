Can it handle after-hours client calls?
Yes. Frontdesk answers 24/7, screens potential clients, collects case details, and books consultations, all while your firm is off the clock.
How does it handle urgent legal matters?
The AI identifies urgency automatically. Court deadlines, opposing party contact, restraining orders, and routes immediately to the appropriate attorney or on-call team.
Does it reduce consultation no-shows?
Yes. Automated confirmation emails and reminder texts go out before every consultation. When a no-show occurs, it immediately calls to reschedule. Recovering up to 40% of missed appointments.
Does it work with existing legal software?
Yes. Frontdesk integrates with Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther, and other practice management tools. Data syncs automatically with no manual entry.
Can it handle multiple practice areas or offices?
Yes. The AI knows all practice areas, attorney availability, and case specialties. Routing accurately across multiple locations and team members simultaneously.
How fast can we get set up?
Most law firms are live in under 5 minutes. Keep your existing phone number. Your AI receptionist starts screening and booking immediately.
