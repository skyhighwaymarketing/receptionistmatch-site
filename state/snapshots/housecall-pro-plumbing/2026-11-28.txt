Frequently asked questions
Is Housecall Pro for residential or commercial plumbing contractors?
Yes, both. Housecall Pro is built for residential and commercial plumbing contractors, with the back-office workflows commercial accounts require.
Manage the work: track orders and the equipment and assets you service across every property
Bill the commercial way: invoice large jobs in stages with progress invoicing, and batch-invoice recurring accounts
Get paid on terms: accept ACH, card on file, and mobile check deposit, and sync everything to QuickBooks
How is Housecall Pro different from enterprise plumbing software?
Housecall Pro gives growing plumbing businesses the capabilities usually locked inside expensive enterprise platforms, without the enterprise price tag, the months-long setup, or the steep learning curve. You get dispatching, route optimization, service agreements, a sales pipeline, AI pricing insights, and reporting in one easy-to-use app. Most Pros are up and running in days, not weeks or months, and the software scales with you from a single truck to a team of 50 or more.
Can Housecall Pro help me book more service and emergency calls?
Yes. Let customers book 24/7 from Google or your website and never miss calls with CSR AI or dedicated call answering. The faster you respond, the more jobs you win, especially on emergency calls.
What is plumbing business management software?
Plumbing business management software from Housecall Pro is a digital tool that streamlines admin tasks, so you can handle more jobs, coordinate your team, and keep customers happy.
Schedule jobs and manage appointments, keeping your team on track and clients informed in real time.
Dispatch your team instantly, assign jobs based on availability, and monitor job progress from anywhere.
Send invoices and accept payments quickly with secure, integrated options to streamline your billing process.
What are the benefits of plumbing business software?
Plumbing business software like Housecall Pro automates routine tasks like scheduling and billing, so you can focus on what you do best.
Improve customer experience with timely updates, easy communication, and secure payment options.
Access all the tools you need on one easy-to-use platform—no more juggling spreadsheets or multiple apps.
Grow your business with flexible features designed to scale and adapt alongside your needs.

What is the best business software for plumbers?
The best plumbing software is the one that fits your business—your team size, workflows, and the way you serve your customers. Look for software that helps you stay organized in the field and in the office, with tools for scheduling, dispatching, invoicing, reporting, and customer management all in one place.
The right software should make it easier to manage plumbing jobs, communicate with customers, and get paid faster—whether you’re a one-truck operation or a growing team with multiple technicians.
Thousands of businesses benefit from using a field service management solution like Housecall Pro, built for plumbing, electrical, and HVAC contractors.
Is Housecall Pro for residential or commercial plumbing contractors?
Housecall Pro is built for both residential and commercial plumbing contractors. Whether you manage service calls for homeowners or large-scale commercial jobs, the software helps you organize every part of your business in one place.
Streamline scheduling, dispatching, invoicing, and reporting while improving communication between office and field staff. With better coordination and visibility across your team, you can reduce errors, save time, and boost profitability.
Thousands of plumbing businesses rely on Housecall Pro’s plumbing software to improve efficiency, stay on top of projects, and grow with confidence.
How can plumbing field service software help grow my business?
Plumbing field service software from Housecall Pro helps your business grow by automating the day-to-day tasks that slow you down. Instead of juggling paperwork or chasing calls, jobs are organized digitally, making it easier to manage teams, track work orders, and get paid faster.
For residential and commercial plumbing companies, field service software improves team communication, keeps customers informed with real-time updates, and reduces scheduling errors that lead to lost revenue. Automated reminders, digital estimates, and online payments make it simple to close more jobs and deliver a professional customer experience.
Whether you’re a solo plumber or a growing business, plumbing software scales with you, freeing up time so you can focus on higher-value tasks like sales, maintenance agreements, and expanding your client base.
