Can I change my plan?
Yes, you can! There’s no charge to change your plan, and we encourage you to plan ahead and work out what you need on a month-by-month basis. We’ll help you to monitor your usage and highlight the best plan for you, whether that means upgrading or downgrading. This means you can adjust to seasonal changes and respond to marketing success.
Why do you bill by the minute?
We bill by number of minutes, instead of number of calls. We find this model suits our clients better, as it allows our receptionists to spend the required time on calls that matter to you. Detailed legal intake takes time, and we ensure that we get all the necessary information from your leads before ending the call.
We time the call from the moment our receptionists pick up until the call is ended, and round up to the minute — a call lasting 2 minutes 28 seconds is recorded as 3 minutes.
What integrations are supported?
LEX Reception integrates with leading legal CRMs and case management software, including Clio, MyCase, LawPay, PracticePanther, Filevine, Lawmatics, Smokeball, CosmoLex and CASEpeer. New contacts and messages go straight into your system, with no need to change settings or reassign messages.
Are there any hidden fees?
No. Each plan shows its monthly price, any setup fee and its per-minute rate for additional minutes. Optional add-ons, such as call transcription and international transfers, are billed separately and listed under “Additional fees apply”.
Do I have to sign a contract?
Plans are billed monthly, and you can change your plan at any time at no charge as your call volume changes.
