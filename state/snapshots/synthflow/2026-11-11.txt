How Is a Synthflow Enterprise Contract Scoped?
Synthflow scopes each Enterprise contract around your call volume and concurrency, telephony and number ownership, integrations, security and procurement requirements, and launch support. Bring those requirements to your scoping call
so the team can price the deployment accurately.

Is Synthflow Free to Use?
No. Synthflow has no free plan or trial and no rate by the minute. It is sold as an annual enterprise agreement, scoped to your call volume, phone system, integrations, security needs and launch support.
Can BPOs and Software Platforms Run Synthflow Under Their Own Brand?
Yes, with white-label in the annual enterprise agreement. You give each client its own workspace under your brand and domain, and see every client in one analytics dashboard. The agreement is scoped to your call volume, phone system, integrations, security needs and launch support.
Which Compliance Standards Does Synthflow Cover?
Synthflow's Trust Center lists SOC 2 Type 2, ISO/IEC 27001, GDPR, HIPAA (self-attested) and PCI DSS v4.0.1, and shows ISO/IEC 42001:2023, DORA and the EU AI Act as in progress. Request the reports in the Trust Center
