How Is Synthflow Priced?
Synthflow is sold as an annual enterprise agreement, scoped to your call volume, phone system, integrations, security needs and launch support. It has no free plan or trial and no rate by the minute.
Synthflow is built for mid-market and enterprise teams deploying AI voice agents for customer calls in customer service, sales and support, from lead qualification to customer support, and every new deployment starts on an Enterprise contract.
The price is set for the year against the call volume you expect, so you can budget for the year before you launch.
