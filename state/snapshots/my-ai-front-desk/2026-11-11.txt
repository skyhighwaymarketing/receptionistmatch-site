FAQ
AI receptionist pricing questions, answered
Everything you need to know about AI receptionist pricing, the AI workforce included in every plan, usage credits, billing, and upgrades.
Contact support
How much does an AI receptionist cost?
A Frontdesk plan that answers phone calls costs $99 per month on Business-in-a-Box, or $79 per month billed annually, and includes 200 voice minutes alongside chat, SMS, CRM, and automations. Partner and Enterprise pricing is custom and set by monthly volume.
How much does an AI receptionist cost per month?
Frontdesk bills $99 per month for Business-in-a-Box, with no contract and no per-seat or per-call fees. Paying annually takes 20% off, which brings it to $79 per month billed upfront. Business-in-a-Box also carries a pool of 1,000 overage credits each month, and auto-reload adds 1,000 more for $10 if you run out.
How much do virtual receptionists cost?
Published prices for AI virtual receptionists start at around $14 per month and rise with the minutes included. ai-receptionist.com advertises plans from $14 per month, Allo from $18 per month, AgentZap from $25 per month, and NextPhone between $199 and $299 per month. Frontdesk charges $99 per month for Business-in-a-Box, the plan that answers phone calls, or $79 per month billed annually.
What is the cheapest AI receptionist?
The cheapest advertised plans in this category sit under $20 per month, with ai-receptionist.com from $14 per month and Allo from $18 per month. Entry tiers are limited by their minute allowance, so compare what each plan includes rather than the headline price alone. The lowest Frontdesk plan that answers calls is Business-in-a-Box at $99 per month, which includes 200 voice minutes plus chat, SMS, CRM, and automations rather than voice alone.
Is there a free AI receptionist?
Frontdesk does not offer a permanently free plan. Paid plans start at $99 per month for Business-in-a-Box, which is the entry point for answering calls and includes 200 voice minutes plus chat, SMS, CRM, and automations. If you are comparing against a free tier elsewhere, check how many minutes or calls it actually includes.
Is an AI receptionist worth it?
It depends on how many calls you currently miss and what one booked customer is worth to you. Business-in-a-Box costs a fixed $99 per month and covers nights, weekends, and holidays, so the comparison to run is that figure against the revenue sitting in calls that currently go to voicemail. A business with low call volume and full phone coverage during opening hours gets less from it than one whose phone rings outside staffed hours.
What does an AI receptionist cost for a small business?
Business-in-a-Box at $99 per month is the plan built for small businesses, or $79 per month billed annually. It covers 1 to 10 interactions per day and includes 200 voice minutes, 100 chat conversations, 400 SMS messages, 500 email drafts, and 300 website form submissions each month. Businesses running more than 25 interactions a day move to Partner or Enterprise pricing, which is quoted on volume.
What happens if I use more than my included voice minutes?
Business-in-a-Box includes 200 voice minutes a month; after that, each extra minute draws 25 credits ($0.25) from the plan's monthly pool of 1,000 overage credits, which covers about 40 more minutes. Unused pool credits expire at the end of the month. If the pool runs out, auto-reload adds 1,000 credits for $10, and purchased credits never expire. Partner and Enterprise plans pay a lower per-minute rate.
Which Frontdesk plan includes API access and integrations?
Every plan connects to calendars, CRMs and thousands of other apps through Zapier, including Business-in-a-Box at $99 per month. Direct API access, custom work, priority support and a dedicated account manager are part of Partner and Enterprise, which are priced on volume. There are no separate Starter or Pro tiers.
