Questions, answered
Is AI included in every plan?
Yes. Every plan includes a 24/7 XBert AI receptionist with 10 free XBert interactions, and persistent customer memory from NextIQ.
What add-ons are there?
Three: an AI Super Pack (unlimited NextIQ transcripts, AI summaries and AI assistant requests, AI scoring for every conversation, and revenue and churn risk alerts), a Messaging Super Pack (unlimited WhatsApp Business messaging and SMS and MMS, in one inbox), and a CX Super Pack, designed around your business.
Can different people on my team have different plans?
Yes. One organization can mix plans across different roles and workflows.
Can I keep my business numbers?
Yes. Business numbers and number porting are included on every plan.
What does “coming soon” mean?
Capabilities marked coming soon or on roadmap aren’t available yet. Everything else on this page is part of the plan it’s listed under.
