FAQS
What are Abby Minutes?
Abby Minutes are how usage is measured on every plan, and they cover both human and AI answering. One minute of human answering uses one Abby Minute, and one minute of AI answering uses half an Abby Minute. So the more you lean on AI, the further your plan stretches, up to twice as far.
What happens if I go over my Abby Minutes?
No problem at all. Any usage beyond your plan is simply billed at your plan’s Abby Minute rate, so there are never any surprises. And if you find you need a little more room each month, you’re welcome to move up a plan anytime, with no penalty and no waiting period.
Is there a free trial or setup fee?
Every plan comes with a free trial, so you can experience Abby before you commit. And there’s no setup fee, getting started is simple and self-serve. See how the free trial works
.
Do I have to sign a long-term contract?
Nope. Abby is month to month, so you’re free to adjust your plan or your mix of human and AI as your needs change. No long-term commitment required.
