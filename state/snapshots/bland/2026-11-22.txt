Frequently asked questions
How do I get started?
Sign up on the Start plan and you get two credits plus an inbound number valued at $15/month, with no card required.
How does Bland's pricing compare to platforms like Vapi or Retell?
Vapi lists $0.05 a minute for hosting and Retell lists $0.055 a minute for voice infrastructure, as of November 2026, and each bills its language model, transcription, and speech on top. Bland's per-minute rate covers its language model, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech in one number, with no token charges and no model-provider pass-throughs. Phone is billed separately, on a customer's own carrier or Bland's at pass-through cost.
Can we bring our own telephony provider?
Yes. Bland integrates with your existing Twilio account, with SIP trunks from any contact-center or telephony provider, or you can use Bland's built-in Twilio at pass-through cost. BYOT customers do not pay transfer fees. You handle carrier costs directly and Bland charges only the per-minute AI rate.
What does Bland cost in total?
Bland's per-minute rate covers its language model, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech: $0.14 a minute on Start, a self-serve plan with no platform fee, or $0.12 a minute on Build, a self-serve plan with a $299 monthly platform fee. Transfer time is $0.05 a minute on Start and $0.04 on Build. Phone is billed separately, on a customer's own carrier or Bland's at pass-through cost. There are no token charges. Enterprise pricing is custom, set with Bland's sales team.
Is Bland HIPAA, SOC 2, GDPR, and PCI compliant?
Yes. SOC 2 Type II (independently audited), HIPAA compliant with a signed BAA, GDPR compliant with a DPA, and PCI DSS v4.0.1 self-attested. Bland's FedRAMP status: FedRAMP 20x Class C (Moderate), certified 17 November 2026, FedRAMP Marketplace FR2628647242. Compliance documentation is available under NDA on Enterprise.
Who runs the implementation?
Our forward-deployed engineering team works directly with yours to build, optimize, and scale the deployment. Enterprise plans include FDE support.
