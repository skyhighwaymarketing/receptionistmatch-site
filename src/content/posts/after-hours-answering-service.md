If you want a live person answering nights, weekends and holidays, [AnswerHero](https://answerhero.com/pricing-plans/) charges $79 a month for 50 minutes and says it adds no weekend or holiday fees. If software is enough, [Upfirst](https://www.upfirst.ai/pricing) can be set to answer after hours only, from $24.95 a month for 30 calls.

Prices come from each vendor's own pricing page, checked November 13, 2026.

An AI receptionist is software that answers your phone. An answering service is live people. Both can cover nights. The big difference is what happens when the 2 a.m. call is a real emergency.

## How after-hours-only use is billed

If you only forward nights and weekends, your volume is small. That makes plan minimums and pay-as-you-go plans matter more than per-minute rates.

- **Smith.ai** says its per-call pricing is the same whether it answers 24/7 or two hours a day. Calls outside your set hours can go to a number you choose or to voicemail.
- **PATLive** bills only for time a receptionist is on your call, not after-call notes. It also supports ring-no-answer forwarding, so calls ring you first.
- **Upfirst** lets you pick every call, overflow only, or after hours on every plan.
- **Rosie** lists the question "Can I start using Rosie for after-hours and overflow calls only?" but the pricing page doesn't show the answer.
- **Abby Connect** calls its $164 Starter plan "best for small offices catching overflow or after-hours calls." A minute of AI answering uses half an Abby Minute.

When does pay-as-you-go stop making sense?

- **PATLive:** past about 17 minutes a month, the $99 plan beats $49 plus $2.99 a minute.
- **Posh:** past about 28 minutes, the $130 plan for 50 minutes beats $65 plus $2.30 a minute.
- **AnswerHero:** past about 30 minutes, the $79 plan beats the $39 plan with 10 minutes and $1.99 per extra minute.

Continental Message Solution bills every 28 days, which means 13 bills a year. Its page also says setup fees vary. For every plan size, see [answering service cost](/answering-service-cost/).

## What happens on a 2 a.m. emergency

A night call ends in one of three ways.

1. **Transfer to your phone.** This only works if someone picks up. Upfirst's waterfall transfers try one person after another, on every plan. Rosie's waterfall starts at $299.
2. **Page the on-call person.** PATLive includes emergency dispatch on every plan. AnswerHero lists call dispatching. Smith.ai sends a text or Slack ping for $0.50 a call, plus $0.25 for each extra person it tries. Continental Message Solution has a real-time on-call portal.
3. **Take a message.** Rosie's $49 plan takes messages but doesn't list transfers. Dialzara's $29 plan takes messages and does blind transfers, but leaves out its "after-hours rules & escalation" feature. For Answrr, the pricing page doesn't say.

Before you sign, ask each vendor four things: what counts as an emergency, who decides, how many people get tried, and what happens if nobody answers.

## Who should keep a live person for nights

- HVAC and plumbing shops where someone has to judge whether a no-heat call is worth a truck roll. See our [HVAC answering service guide](/hvac-answering-service/).
- Dental and medical offices whose night callers are in pain or scared.
- Law firms taking urgent intake calls.
- Any business whose on-call tech doesn't reliably answer transfers.

AnswerHero has the lowest live entry prices and lists call dispatching on all plans. PATLive spells out emergency dispatch on every plan. Smith.ai fits if you want to accept or reject each transfer.

**Not for you if** your night calls are mostly booking requests and "are you open Saturday?" You'd pay live rates, like $1.89 a minute past AnswerHero's Starter plan or $2.99 on PATLive's pay-as-you-go plan, for answers software can give.

## When an AI receptionist covers nights

Upfirst fits a shop that gets a few calls a night and has an on-call phone that gets answered. Smith.ai's free AI plan covers 25 calls a month, and hard calls can go to a Smith.ai live receptionist. VoiceNation's AI plans, from $99 for 50 calls, include "human escalation," but the page doesn't say whose staff takes the call.

**Not for you if** a wrong call at night costs real money and nobody on your team will answer a transfer. A transfer to a sleeping tech is voicemail with extra steps. To see which AI plans hand calls to a person, read [AI phone answering services with live backup](/ai-phone-answering-service/).

## How to pick

1. **Pull a month of after-hours calls.** Count them and their minutes. Below about 17 to 30 minutes, depending on the vendor, a pay-as-you-go plan is cheaper.
2. **Count real emergencies.** If there are almost none, an AI receptionist that takes messages will do.
3. **Test it at night.** Call at 10 p.m. and fake an emergency. Use a trial from our [AI receptionist free trial guide](/ai-receptionist-free-trial/).
4. **Still unsure?** Compare [AI receptionist vs answering service](/ai-receptionist-vs-answering-service/).

**Who this page is NOT for:** businesses that need someone on site overnight. These services answer calls. They don't open doors.
