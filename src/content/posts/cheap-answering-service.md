The cheapest live answering service with a published price is [AnswerHero](https://answerhero.com/pricing-plans/). Its Economy plan costs $39 a month for 10 minutes of human answering, then $1.99 a minute.

Prices come from each vendor's own pricing page, checked November 15, 2026.

An answering service means live people answer your phone. An AI receptionist is software. This page ranks live services first. Software costs less, and it gets its own section below.

When should you leave the no-minimum plan for a bigger one?

- **AnswerHero:** past about 30 minutes, Starter beats Economy.
- **PATLive:** past about 17 minutes, the 50-minute plan beats pay as you go.
- **Posh:** past about 28 minutes, Vogue beats Chïc.

## What the cheap tier leaves out

- **AnswerHero Economy** lists "Basic Features." Every other human plan lists "Advanced Features." The pricing page doesn't say what Basic leaves out, so ask before you sign. Ten minutes is also only about 4 calls.
- **PATLive pay as you go** has the same features as every PATLive plan, including emergency dispatch and 24/7 answering. The catch is the rate. At $2.99 a minute it's the highest published extra-minute rate in the table. PATLive bills only time a receptionist is on your call, not after-call notes. Prices are plus taxes and fees.
- **Continental Message Solution** doesn't publish its extra-minute rate. Setup fees "vary depending on account complexity." It bills every 28 days, so you get 13 bills a year. That's about $650 a year before any minutes.
- **Posh Chïc** transfers callers to you only when your app status allows. Its receptionist minutes also count time spent on tasks for your account, not just talk time.
- **Smith.ai** charges $0.50 a call for a text or Slack ping to accept a transfer, on top of $300 a month.

## Cheaper still: an AI receptionist

If your calls are mostly hours, prices and booking, software costs a fraction of this.

- **[AnswerHero AI](https://answerhero.com/pricing-plans/):** $19 a month, $0.79 per extra minute. The pricing page doesn't say how many minutes are included.
- **[Smith.ai AI Receptionist](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist):** $0 for 25 calls, then $3.00 a call. Hard calls can go to a Smith.ai live receptionist.
- **[Upfirst](https://www.upfirst.ai/pricing):** $24.95 for 30 calls, then $1.50 a call. Warm transfers to your team on every plan.

Full plan tables are in [how much an AI receptionist costs](/ai-receptionist-cost/). To weigh software against people, read [AI receptionist vs answering service](/ai-receptionist-vs-answering-service/).

## Who each cheap option is for

**AnswerHero Economy** fits a business with a few calls a month that wants a person, not software. **Not for you if** you use more than about 30 minutes. Starter costs less from there.

**PATLive pay as you go** fits a shop with rare calls where an emergency has to be dispatched by a person. It has a 14-day free trial. **Not for you if** you go past about 17 minutes a month.

**Posh Chïc** fits an owner who answers some calls and wants a person to catch the rest. Its free trial is 7 days or 250 minutes, no card. **Not for you if** you want a flat, predictable bill.

**Continental Message Solution** fits someone happy to get a quote by phone. **Not for you if** you want the extra-minute rate in writing before you sign.

## How to pick

1. **Count last month's calls and minutes.** If you don't track minutes, multiply calls by 2.5.
2. **Under about 30 minutes:** AnswerHero Economy costs least at every volume in the table above. Pick PATLive pay as you go (under about 17 minutes) if you want its listed emergency dispatch, or Posh Chïc (under about 28 minutes) if you want app-based transfers.
3. **Around 20 calls:** a 50-minute plan is cheaper. AnswerHero Starter has the lowest price for one.
4. **Ask two questions:** what the cheap plan leaves out, and the extra-minute rate.

For every plan size, see [answering service cost](/answering-service-cost/). If you only need nights and weekends, see [after-hours answering service prices](/after-hours-answering-service/).

**Who this page is NOT for:** businesses with hundreds of calls a month. At that volume the entry price doesn't matter much. Compare the per-minute rate on bigger plans instead.
