**Short answer:** most small businesses need one of two things. **A live answering service** (real people) starts around $99 to $165 a month for 50 minutes: [PATLive](https://www.patlive.com/pricing/) at $99, [Posh](https://www.posh.com/pricing) at $130, [Abby Connect](https://www.abby.com/pricing/) at $164. **An AI receptionist** starts around $25 to $50 a month for far more calls: [Answrr](https://tryanswrr.com/pricing) at $49 for 250 minutes. Pick live people if your callers need judgment. Pick AI if most calls are booking, hours and pricing questions.

Prices below come from each service's own pricing page, checked November 11, 2026.

## Step 1: decide between live people and AI

| If your calls are mostly... | Choose |
|---|---|
| Booking, hours, directions, "do you do X?" | AI receptionist |
| Upset customers, emergencies that need a judgment call | Live answering service |
| Legal or medical intake with sensitive details | Live answering service |
| After-hours overflow you just need captured | Either; AI costs less |

Live people cost about **$2 to $5 a minute** at entry level. AI receptionists cost about **$0.20 to $0.50 a minute**. The full breakdown is in [AI receptionist vs answering service](/ai-receptionist-vs-answering-service/).

## Step 2: size your plan from your call log

Look at last month's calls before you shop. PATLive's pricing page estimates 50 minutes as "about 20 calls", so a typical call runs about 2.5 minutes.

| Calls a month | Minutes to plan for | Live service cost | AI receptionist cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| About 20 | 50 | $99 (PATLive) to $250 (Ruby) | $24.95 (Upfirst, 30 calls) |
| About 80 | 200 | $349 (PATLive) to $720 (Ruby) | $49 (Answrr or Rosie, 250 min) |
| About 200 | 500 | $759 (PATLive) to $1,725 (Ruby) | $99 (Answrr Growth, 700 min) |

Bigger plans generally cost less per minute. See the full price table in [answering service cost](/answering-service-cost/), and AI plan details in [how much an AI receptionist costs](/ai-receptionist-cost/).

## Step 3: check the terms that cost money later

- **Overage rate.** PATLive charges $2.29 a minute past its 50-minute plan, VoiceNation $2.85. Ruby's page doesn't state one.
- **Billing cycle.** [Continental Message Solution](https://www.continentalmessage.com/pricing/) bills every 28 days, so 13 bills a year.
- **Contract.** PATLive and VoiceNation say no contract. Continental Message Solution is month to month with a 28-day cancellation policy.
- **Setup fee.** Abby Connect and Ruby say none.

## Who each type is NOT for

- **Live answering is NOT for** an owner on a tight budget whose calls are mostly simple. You pay for a person's minutes even when the question is "are you open Saturday?"
- **An AI receptionist is NOT for** a business where a wrong answer is costly, such as emergency triage or legal intake, unless it hands those calls to a person.
- **Neither is for** a business that needs someone physically at the front desk.
