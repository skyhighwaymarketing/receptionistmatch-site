**Short answer:** most AI receptionists for a small business cost **$25 to $100 a month** for the starting plan. The cheapest we found is [Upfirst at $24.95 a month for 30 calls](https://www.upfirst.ai/pricing). [Answrr](https://tryanswrr.com/pricing) and [Rosie](https://heyrosie.com/pricing) both start at $49 a month for 250 minutes. A live answering service costs far more: [Ruby](https://www.ruby.com/pricing/) starts at $250 a month for 50 minutes.

The price that matters is what you pay at *your* call volume, because every vendor counts differently: minutes, calls, or unique callers. The tables below use each vendor's own pricing page, checked November 11, 2026.

## What 500 minutes a month really costs

Starting prices hide the real bill. If your phone rings 15 to 20 times a day, you can use 500 minutes a month. Here is what each minute-based vendor charges at that volume, worked out from their own plan limits and overage rates:

| Vendor | Cheapest way to cover 500 minutes | Monthly cost |
|---|---|---|
| [Answrr](https://tryanswrr.com/pricing) | Growth plan, 700 minutes | $99 |
| [Rosie](https://heyrosie.com/pricing) | Scale plan, 1,000 minutes | $149 |
| [My AI Front Desk](https://www.myaifrontdesk.com/pricing) | 200 minutes + 300 extra at $0.25 | $174 |
| [Dialzara](https://www.dialzara.com/pricing) | Business Plus, 500 minutes | $199 |
| [Ruby](https://www.ruby.com/pricing/) (live people) | 500-minute plan | $1,725 |

Call-based vendors (Upfirst, Smith.ai) and caller-based ones (Goodcall) don't fit this table without guessing how long your calls run. If your average call is about 3 minutes, 500 minutes is roughly 165 calls: Upfirst's Pro plan covers 300 calls for $159.95 a month, and Smith.ai's AI Pro covers 150 calls for $270 a month plus $2.30 per extra call.

## AI receptionist vs live answering service cost

Live receptionists charge for a person's time, so the gap is large.

| Service | Type | Starting price | Included |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Ruby](https://www.ruby.com/pricing/) | Live people | $250/mo | 50 minutes |
| [Smith.ai](https://smith.ai/pricing) | Live people | $300/mo | 30 calls ($11.50 per extra call) |
| [Answrr](https://tryanswrr.com/pricing) | AI | $49/mo | 250 minutes |

At the entry level, a live service costs about **five to ten times more per minute or call**. What you get for it is judgment: a person can calm an upset caller, handle an odd request, or decide a call is urgent. If most of your calls are booking, pricing questions and messages, an AI receptionist covers them for a fraction of the cost. For the full side-by-side, see [AI receptionist vs answering service](/ai-receptionist-vs-answering-service/), and for every live plan size, [answering service cost](/answering-service-cost/).

## Developer platforms: cheap per minute, but you build it

[Retell](https://www.retellai.com/pricing) ($0.07 to $0.31 a minute), [Vapi](https://vapi.ai/pricing) ($0.05 a minute for hosting, with voice, model and phone costs billed on top) and [Bland](https://www.bland.ai/pricing) ($0.14 a minute on its Start plan) sell the parts to build your own voice agent. The per-minute price looks low, but someone has to set up the prompts, phone numbers and integrations.

**Not for:** an owner who wants calls answered this week without hiring a developer. Pick one of the ready-made receptionists above instead.

## Costs that catch people out

- **The billing unit.** Minutes, calls and unique callers are not the same thing. A 30-call plan can run out in a week if your phone is busy; a 250-minute plan lasts longer if your calls are short.
- **Add-ons on top of a base plan.** Jobber, GoHighLevel and Nextiva's per-conversation option all need a software plan first. Count both.
- **Overage rates.** Dialzara's $0.48 a minute on its $29 plan is almost twice Answrr's $0.25. If you go over every month, move up a plan.
- **Annual billing.** My AI Front Desk drops to $79 a month and Upfirst to $20 a month if you pay for the year. Only lock in once the trial proves it works for your calls.

| Your situation | Where to look |

| A steady 5 to 15 calls a day | Answrr or Rosie at $49 for 250 minutes |
| You already run Jobber | The $29 Jobber receptionist add-on |
| Callers need a real person (legal intake, upset customers) | A live service; see our [answering service guide for small businesses](/answering-service-for-small-business/) |

**Who this page is NOT for:** call centers and businesses handling thousands of calls a month. At that size every vendor here quotes enterprise pricing, and you should get three quotes.
