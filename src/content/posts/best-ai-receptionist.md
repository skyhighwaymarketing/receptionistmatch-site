An AI receptionist is software that answers your phone. An answering service is live people. This page covers software only. The right pick depends on the job you need done, so start with the job.

## Booking into your calendar

Upfirst books into Google, Outlook, Clio, Acuity, Calendly, Square and more, on every plan. Extra calls cost $1.50 on Starter. Spam and calls under 15 seconds aren't billed. Rosie books onto your calendar only from its $149 Scale plan. Its $49 plan texts a booking link instead. Dialzara's Google and Outlook sync starts at $99.

**Not for you if** you want to stay on the $24.95 Starter plan and take well over 30 calls a month. That's about one a day. Past that, Upfirst's Premium plan is $59.95 for 90 calls.

## Qualifying leads

Smith.ai lists lead qualification, call routing and native integrations with Clio and Zapier on its free plan. Free covers 25 calls, then $3.00 per extra call. Pro is $150 for 75 calls, then $2.50 each. It fits law firms and anyone whose leads need screening questions.

**Not for you if** you get lots of short, low-value calls. Smith.ai's page lists Pro at $2.00 a call. Minute-based plans cost less for those. Canceling also takes 30 days' notice.

## Answering FAQs after hours

Rosie is $49 for 250 minutes, 24/7. It learns from your Google profile and website, and you can add custom FAQ answers. Rosie's page doesn't say what overage costs. [Answrr](https://tryanswrr.com/pricing) is also $49 for 250 minutes, with top-ups at $0.25 a minute. Its pricing page says every plan includes every feature but doesn't list them. For nights-only setups, see our [after-hours answering service guide](/after-hours-answering-service/).

**Not for you if** after-hours callers sometimes need you. Rosie's transfers start on the $149 plan.

## Handing a caller to a real person

Smith.ai runs its AI first. Its page says a complex call "can be transferred to a live North America-based receptionist," day or night. Upfirst does warm and waterfall transfers to your own team on every plan. Waterfall means it tries one person after another. Compare every option in [AI phone answering services with live backup](/ai-phone-answering-service/).

**Not for you if** you pick Upfirst and nobody on your team answers transfers. Then the call goes to voicemail anyway.

## Texting callers

None of these pricing pages lists an automatic missed-call text-back by name. An AI receptionist answers the call first, so fewer calls are missed. If texting matters, My AI Front Desk's $99 plan includes 400 texts, two-way SMS and follow-up sequences, like texting a new lead and then emailing the next day. Extra texts cost 4 credits, about $0.04 each. Upfirst includes two-way texting on every plan.

**Not for you if** you only want your phone answered. My AI Front Desk's plan bundles chat, a CRM and texting.

## Bilingual callers

Upfirst switches between 35+ languages automatically, then translates the transcript into yours. Rosie handles English and Spanish on every call. Dialzara speaks 10 languages on every plan. Details are in our [bilingual answering service guide](/bilingual-answering-service/).

**Not for you if** you need certified medical or legal interpreting. None of these pages mention it.

## Working inside a field-service or CRM tool

Jobber Receptionist answers calls and texts 24/7 and books jobs into your Jobber calendar. It's a $99 add-on on Core, Connect or Grow, and included in Plus at $499. On Core ($49, no commitment) that's about $148 a month. The page doesn't say how many calls it covers or whether it hands callers to a person. Housecall Pro's CSR AI shows no price. GoHighLevel's AI Employee is $50 a month per sub-account on top of the $97 Starter plan, plus usage charges. [RingCentral](https://www.ringcentral.com/ai-receptionist.html) starts at $39 a month and names Salesforce, HubSpot and Zoho. HVAC shops should read our [HVAC answering service guide](/hvac-answering-service/). Property managers, see our [property management answering service guide](/answering-service-for-property-management/).

**Not for you if** you aren't already on that software. Don't switch systems just to get a receptionist.

## Jobs no AI receptionist does well yet

- **Judgment calls.** These pages describe rules you set, not judgment. Deciding whether a 2 a.m. no-heat call is worth a truck roll still needs a person.
- **Scared or upset callers.** A calm human voice still does this better.
- **Sensitive intake.** Upfirst lists HIPAA BAAs under its Enterprise plan, not its every-plan features. Check before medical calls go to software.
- **Calls with nobody to transfer to.** A transfer to a phone nobody answers is voicemail.

For these, compare [AI receptionist vs answering service](/ai-receptionist-vs-answering-service/). Vapi, Retell, Bland and Synthflow sell parts to build your own voice agent. They're out of scope here.
