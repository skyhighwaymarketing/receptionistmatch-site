**Short answer:** a live answering service costs about **$100 to $250 a month for 50 minutes**, which works out to roughly **$2 to $5 a minute**. The lowest entry plan we found is [PATLive at $99 a month for 50 minutes](https://www.patlive.com/pricing/). [Ruby](https://www.ruby.com/pricing/) is at the top of the range at $250 for 50 minutes. Busier offices pay $350 to $720 a month for 200 minutes.

These are live people answering your phone. If most of your calls are booking and simple questions, an AI receptionist costs a fraction of this; see [how much an AI receptionist costs](/ai-receptionist-cost/). Prices below come from each service's own pricing page, checked November 11, 2026.

## Pay-as-you-go plans

If you get only a handful of calls, three services sell a plan with no minutes included:

- [PATLive](https://www.patlive.com/pricing/): $49 a month plus $2.99 a minute.
- [Posh](https://www.posh.com/pricing): $65 a month plus $2.30 a minute.
- [Continental Message Solution](https://www.continentalmessage.com/pricing/): $50 every 28 days, at your plan's additional-minute rate.

At about 20 minutes a month or more, PATLive's 50-minute plan becomes cheaper than its pay-as-you-go plan.

## Costs that catch people out

- **28-day billing.** Continental Message Solution bills every 28 days, which is 13 bills a year, not 12. Its $575 plan costs about $623 a month in calendar terms.
- **Minutes vs calls.** PATLive estimates 50 minutes as "about 20 calls", which is 2.5 minutes a call. If your calls run longer, you need a bigger plan.
- **Overage you can't see.** Abby Connect bills overage at your plan's minute rate, and Ruby's page doesn't state one. Ask before you sign.
- **Setup fees.** Abby Connect and Ruby say there is no setup fee. Abby, PATLive and Posh offer free trials.

## Which plan size fits you

| Your calls | Where to start |
|---|---|
| A few calls a week | A pay-as-you-go plan (PATLive $49, Posh $65) |
| About 1 to 2 calls a day | A 50-minute plan, $99 to $250 |
| 5 to 10 calls a day | A 200-minute plan, $349 to $720 |
| Mostly simple calls, any volume | An AI receptionist; compare [AI receptionist vs answering service](/ai-receptionist-vs-answering-service/) |
