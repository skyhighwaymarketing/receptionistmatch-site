**Short answer:** most AI phone answering services start at **$25 to $100 a month**, and the big difference between them is what happens when the AI can't help. Only two vendors' pricing pages say they hand the call to their own live receptionists: [Smith.ai](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist) (free for 25 calls, $150 for 75) and [Abby Connect](https://www.abby.com/pricing/) (from $164 a month). Most others, like [Rosie](https://heyrosie.com/pricing) and [Dialzara](https://www.dialzara.com/pricing), transfer the caller to your phone instead.

Prices and features come from each vendor's own pricing page, checked November 11, 2026.

A transfer to your phone only helps if someone answers it. If you're on a roof or under a sink, that call goes to voicemail, the same as before you paid for anything.

## The two that keep a person on standby

**Smith.ai** runs its AI first, and the pricing page says a complex call "can be transferred to a live North America-based receptionist." Plans count calls: free for 25 a month, $150 for 75, $270 for 150, $500 for 300. Extra calls cost $2.10 to $3.00 each, depending on the plan.

**Abby Connect** bills everything in "Abby Minutes." A minute of AI answering uses half an Abby Minute and a minute of a human uses a full one, and a person can step in on the same call. Plans run $164 for 50 Abby Minutes to $1,380 for 500. There's no setup fee and a free trial on every plan. To compare trial length, caps and card requirements across vendors, see our [AI receptionist free trial lookup](/ai-receptionist-free-trial/).

**Not for you if** your calls are simple and you just want them answered cheaply. A plan that only transfers to you costs a fraction of these.

## The ones that transfer to you

These are the cheapest way to get every call answered, with your own phone as the backup.

- **Upfirst** starts at $24.95 a month for 30 calls, with a 14-day free trial and no card. Warm transfers introduce the caller before connecting.
- **Dialzara** starts at $29 for 60 minutes. The $29 plan does blind transfers only; warm transfers start at the $99 Pro plan (220 minutes).
- **Rosie** starts at $49 for 250 minutes, but call transfers start on the $149 Scale plan (1,000 minutes). The $299 Growth plan tries several numbers until someone answers.
- **Goodcall** starts at $79 for 100 unique callers a month and lets you choose who receives transfers.

**Not for you if** nobody can reliably pick up a transferred call during your work day. Look at Smith.ai or Abby instead, or a live answering service.

## How to pick

1. **Count your calls that need a person.** Pull a week of your call log. If fewer than 1 in 10 needs a judgment call, a transfer-to-you plan is enough.
2. **Check what's counted.** Calls, minutes and unique callers price very differently. A 2-minute call costs one call on Upfirst but only 2 minutes of 250 on Rosie.
3. **Read the plan, not the feature list.** Transfers are often missing from the cheapest plan, as with Rosie and Dialzara's warm transfer.
4. **Price the human part.** On Abby a human minute uses twice the allowance of an AI minute. If most calls end up with a person, a live answering service may cost about the same.
