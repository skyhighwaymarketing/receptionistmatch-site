If you want a live person answering in Spanish at no extra charge, [PATLive](https://www.patlive.com/pricing/) starts at $99 a month for 50 minutes and says it has "no bilingual fees." If software is enough, [Upfirst](https://www.upfirst.ai/pricing) answers in 35+ languages from $24.95 a month for 30 calls and translates the transcript into your language.

Prices come from each vendor's own pricing page, checked November 15, 2026.

An answering service means live people answer. An AI receptionist is software. Both can speak Spanish. What you need to know is who picks up, what it costs extra, and whether you can read the call notes afterward.

## Live Spanish speakers: what each one charges

**PATLive** charges nothing extra for Spanish. It includes a free auto attendant so callers can press 1 for English or 2 for Spanish. It bills only time a receptionist is live on your call. It has a 14-day free trial and no contracts.

**Posh** includes bilingual English and Spanish receptionists on every plan. Chïc is $65 a month plus $2.30 a minute. Its minutes count task time for your account, not just talk time. Free trial: 7 days or 250 minutes, no card.

**VoiceNation** answers "Are there extra fees for bilingual answering or call transfers?" with "No." Plans start at $165 for 50 minutes, then $2.85 a minute.

**Abby Connect** lists English and Spanish answering in every plan from $164. The pricing page doesn't say whether Spanish calls go to a person, the AI, or both. Ask. It matters because an AI minute uses half an Abby Minute.

**Ruby** says "24/7 Spanish and bilingual call handling available" and calls bilingual answering an optional feature. It also says its plans differ only by minutes. Confirm Spanish is included before you sign the $250 plan.

**Smith.ai** is the one that charges. A dedicated Spanish line costs $1.00 per call, and every call on it goes to a fully bilingual receptionist. On the $300 Starter plan with 30 calls, that's about $30 more, or about $330 a month.

**Who it's for:** law firms taking intake, dental and medical offices, and HVAC or plumbing shops where a Spanish-speaking caller has an emergency. A person can calm someone down and decide what's urgent.

**Not for you if** most of your Spanish calls are hours, prices and booking. You'd pay live rates, like PATLive's $2.29 extra minute, for answers software gives. For per-minute math on every plan, see [answering service cost](/answering-service-cost/).

## AI receptionists: language count and the transcript question

If you don't speak the caller's language, the notes matter as much as the greeting.

- **Upfirst** is the only page here that says it translates the transcript into your language. It switches languages automatically, on every plan. Extra calls cost $1.50 on Starter.
- **Dialzara** speaks 10 languages on every plan. The $29 plan does blind transfers only. Warm transfers start at $99.
- **Rosie** handles English and Spanish on every call. Transfers start on the $149 plan.
- **My AI Front Desk** lists 20+ languages on its $99 plan with 200 minutes. Extra minutes cost 25 credits, about $0.25.
- **RingCentral** names six languages and says it switches mid-call. The page text shows no price.

**Upfirst is for** an owner whose callers speak a language past Spanish, and who needs to read the notes in English. The page says 35+ languages but doesn't list them, so check yours. **Not for you if** your calls need judgment. For AI plans that hand calls to a person, see [AI phone answering services with live backup](/ai-phone-answering-service/).

**Rosie and Dialzara are for** shops whose calls come in English and Spanish only, with lots of short calls. **Not for you if** you need a third language from Rosie, or warm transfers on Dialzara's cheapest plan.

**Smith.ai's Spanish line is for** a business where most callers speak Spanish. **Not for you if** only a few do. The $1.00 applies to every call routed to that line.

## How to pick

1. **Count your Spanish calls.** Pull a month of call logs. A handful a week points to a pay-as-you-go live plan or an AI receptionist. See the [cheap answering service](/cheap-answering-service/) ranking.
2. **List every language you hear.** If it's only Spanish, all of the live services here can cover it. Beyond Spanish, only the AI pages list more languages.
3. **Ask what language the notes come in.** Only Upfirst answers that on its pricing page.
4. **Test it in Spanish.** Have a Spanish speaker call during a trial and ask a real question. Here's every [AI receptionist free trial](/ai-receptionist-free-trial/).
5. **Still choosing between people and software?** Read [AI receptionist vs answering service](/ai-receptionist-vs-answering-service/). HVAC shops can also check the [HVAC answering service guide](/hvac-answering-service/).

**Who this page is NOT for:** offices that need certified medical or legal interpreting. None of these pricing pages mention interpreter certification. Ask for it in writing.
