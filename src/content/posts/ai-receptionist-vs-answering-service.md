**Short answer:** an AI receptionist costs about **one-tenth as much** as a live answering service. [Answrr](https://tryanswrr.com/pricing) covers 250 minutes for $49 a month; a live service like [PATLive](https://www.patlive.com/pricing/) charges $99 for 50 minutes, and [Ruby](https://www.ruby.com/pricing/) $250. Choose AI when your calls are routine. Choose live people when callers need judgment, comfort or a decision.

Prices come from each vendor's own pricing page, checked November 11, 2026.

## Monthly cost side by side

| Minutes a month | AI receptionist | Live answering service |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | $24.95 to $49 ([Upfirst](https://www.upfirst.ai/pricing), [Answrr](https://tryanswrr.com/pricing), [Rosie](https://heyrosie.com/pricing)) | $99 to $250 ([PATLive](https://www.patlive.com/pricing/), [Ruby](https://www.ruby.com/pricing/)) |
| 200 | $49 (Answrr or Rosie, 250 minutes) | $349 to $720 (PATLive, Ruby) |
| 500 | $99 (Answrr Growth, 700 minutes) to $199 ([Dialzara](https://www.dialzara.com/pricing) Business Plus) | $759 to $1,725 (PATLive, Ruby) |
| Per minute, entry plan | About $0.20 (Answrr, Rosie) to $0.48 (Dialzara) | About $1.98 (PATLive) to $5.00 (Ruby) |

## What each one does well

| | AI receptionist | Live answering service |
|---|---|---|
| Handles an upset or confused caller | Weak | Strong |
| Makes a judgment call (is this urgent?) | Only with rules you set | Yes |
| Cost per minute | $0.20 to $0.50 | $2 to $5 |

## Choose an AI receptionist if

- Most calls are booking, hours, pricing and "do you service my area?"
- You miss calls because you're on a job, not because callers need a person.
- Your budget is under $100 a month. See [how much an AI receptionist costs](/ai-receptionist-cost/).

**Not for you if** a wrong answer is expensive, like emergency triage or legal intake, unless it passes those calls to a person.

## Choose a live answering service if

- Callers are often stressed, sick or upset and need a calm person.
- Calls need a decision a script can't make.
- You can budget $100 to $250 a month for about 20 calls. See [answering service cost](/answering-service-cost/).

**Not for you if** your calls are mostly simple questions. You'll pay $2 or more a minute for answers an AI gives for about $0.20.

## The middle option: both

Some vendors sell both. [Smith.ai](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist) has an AI receptionist from $0 for 25 calls and $150 for 75 calls, next to its live plans from $300 for 30 calls. [VoiceNation](https://www.voicenation.com/pricing/) sells AI plans from $99 for 50 calls alongside live plans from $165 for 50 minutes. One way to use this: let AI take routine calls and send hard ones to a person.
